# Security and ethics

**Author:** Hossein Tabasi  
**Project:** GenFinEcology

## Purpose

Closed experimental loop for synthetic financial discourse under locked fact cards (research / thesis). Extends GenFinTextEval with construct-isolated ablations and ecological rounds.

## Allowed use

- Academic study of faithfulness, belief-update proxies, contamination dynamics, and surface detectability.
- Offline, seedable template generation with declared sourced or lab-labeled fact cards.
- Teaching demonstrations of evaluation design (Layers A/B/C+/D).

## Prohibited use

- Advising or executing market manipulation, wash trading, spoofing, or coordinated deception.
- Publishing fabricated “according to &lt;ORG&gt;” claims as real news.
- Deploying persuasion-steered or authority-cued variants to social platforms as authentic speech.
- Treating computational persona outputs as investment advice or as evidence about named living persons.

## Design mitigations

- Ablation variants are labeled in metadata.
- Fabricated sources are Layer A / D metrics targets, not endorsed attributions.
- Every demo path states texts are not investment advice.
- Lab fixtures, if any, must be marked `lab_fixture` / “not market history.”
- No live posting, brokerage, or order-routing modules.

## Data

Event numbers are copied from previously verified public fact cards (GenFinTextEval / SynthOpinion / EchoMarket lineage), or declared as lab fixtures. Do not invent new ticker events or statistics without sourcing or an explicit lab label.
