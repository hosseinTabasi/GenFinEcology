# Thesis notes — GenFinEcology

**Author:** Hossein Tabasi  
**Program framing:** M.Tech / early-PhD methods chapter

## Positioning

GenFinEcology is the **ablation-and-ecology** successor to GenFinTextEval. Use GenFinTextEval for the compact A/B/C baseline narrative; use this repository for construct attribution, detectability, multi-round contamination, and bootstrap uncertainty.

## Suggested chapter spine

1. Motivation: why blob “persuade” is insufficient (information vs persuasion tension).
2. Protocol: claim taxonomy, units, counterfactuals, threats (PROTOCOL §§1–8).
3. Ablation design and manipulation checks (Layer A table).
4. Belief results by isolate (RQ1–RQ2).
5. Ecological dynamics: ρ sweep vs R-round preferential reuse (RQ3).
6. Surface detectability and held-out transfer (RQ4; descriptive only).
7. Limitations: template family, lexicon polarity, computational personas ≠ humans.
